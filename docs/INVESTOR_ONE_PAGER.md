# Agentic Governance Framework (AGF)

### The Core Premise: Mathematics > Policies
Artificial Intelligence operates probabilistically, but **regulatory law demands deterministic guarantees**. Executive boards currently carry the legal and financial liability for AI outputs across mission-critical sectors. Traditional Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platforms rely on post-facto, retroactive human auditing that is fundamentally incapable of scaling with real-time autonomous AI agents. 

**AGF is the mandatory infrastructure choke-point.** We transfer compliance liability from the corporate board to cryptographic mathematics.

### The Product
AGF is a deterministic, Zero-Knowledge (ZK) compliance engine built on the RISC-V SP1 zkVM. Before any AI agent executes an action—whether placing a trade, accessing a medical record, or altering a drone's flight path—the output is evaluated against deterministic Agentic Rule Specification Language (`.arsl`) schemas. 

*   **Real-Time Guardrails:** Evaluates dozens of complex rules in **< 6 milliseconds**.
*   **Cryptographic Audit Trails:** Every decision generates an Ed25519 digital signature, a Blake3 hash-chain, and a Groth16 ZK-proof verifiable by regulators in 77ms.
*   **Zero Trust Architecture:** No data disclosure required. The proof guarantees the rule was followed exactly as specified without exposing underlying proprietary algorithms or PHI data.
